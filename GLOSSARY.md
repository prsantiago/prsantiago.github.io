# PRsantiago Personal Brand

Public website and client inquiry touchpoint for an independent software and AI/data practitioner serving non-technical SMB founders.

## Language

**PRsantiago**:
The public brand name used in site headers, page titles, metadata, and copyright notices.
_Avoid_: Agency, Studio, Monograph

**Santiago**:
The practitioner's first name used in direct contact links, WhatsApp/email starters, and personal introductions.
_Avoid_: Principal architect, founder and CEO

**SMB Founder**:
A non-technical small-to-medium business owner looking for practical automation, internal software, or data work without managing engineering jargon.
_Avoid_: Enterprise team, corporate stakeholder, engineering lead

**Entry Template**:
A commented-out HTML skeleton stored in `src/projects/` or `src/writing/` for future real entries, kept out of the live rendered page.
_Avoid_: Template Slot, mock case study, placeholder card
