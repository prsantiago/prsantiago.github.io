# PRsantiago Personal Brand

Developer-first personal brand website and public archive for Santiago, publishing technical writing, software projects, and engineering history to establish a voice in the dev industry.

## Language

**PRsantiago**:
The public brand name used in site headers, page titles, metadata, and copyright notices.
_Avoid_: Agency, Studio, Monograph, Consultancy

**Santiago**:
The engineer's first name used in personal introductions and page bios.
_Avoid_: Principal architect, founder and CEO

**Project**:
A software build, tool, or engineering experiment published in the site's portfolio archive.
_Avoid_: Case study, client deliverable, engagement

**Article**:
A technical essay, guide, or engineering note published in the site's archive to share Santiago's perspective in the dev industry.
_Avoid_: Blog post, thought leadership, insight

**Entry Template**:
A commented-out HTML skeleton stored in `src/projects/` or `src/articles/` for future real entries, kept out of the live rendered page.
_Avoid_: Template Slot, mock case study, placeholder card
